# 📝 Task Planner App

Task Planner App is a personal productivity companion designed to help you manage your tasks efficiently while keeping track of your fatigue levels. With our intuitive interface and advanced features, you can ensure a balance between work and personal life.

## ✨ Features

- **👤 Personalized Onboarding**: Enter your name and start with a customized experience.
- **😴 Fatigue Levels**: Monitor your fatigue levels based on task completion.
- **📅 Task Scheduler**: Plan, organize, and manage your daily tasks effortlessly.
- **🔔 Task Notifications**: Receive timely notifications about your tasks and fatigue status.
- **📱 User-Friendly Interface**: Enjoy a clean and intuitive interface for easy navigation and task management.

## 📚 How to Use

1. Launch the Task Planner App on your iOS device.
2. Enter your name during the onboarding process to personalize your experience.
3. View your fatigue levels and understand how different tasks affect them.
4. Use the task scheduler to plan and organize your daily tasks.
5. Receive notifications about your tasks and fatigue status to stay on top of your productivity.
6. Enjoy a balanced and productive lifestyle with Task Planner App!
